Executive Summary

The Challenge & Vision

Managing high-volume Shopify stores with tens of thousands of variants, custom Liquid themes, and heavy media assets creates severe operational bottlenecks. Manual SEO optimization (such as writing descriptive alt-texts and meta-tags for 5,000+ items) is time-prohibitive. Store theme edits made directly in Shopify lack granular version control and instantaneous rollback, while storage costs balloon from unreferenced duplicate assets.

To solve these operational hurdles, I engineered Shopify Agent—a proprietary, standalone web platform that bridges custom business logic, automated AI pipelines, and deep Shopify administrative protocols to give merchants complete autonomy over their stores.

Technical Engineering

Key Engineering Milestones

1. Solving PHP Timeout & Memory Overflows on Enterprise Backups

Generating comprehensive multi-gigabyte ZIP archives with thousands of theme liquid files and high-res media caused execution timeouts in standard environments. I re-architected the backup pipeline into chunked streaming jobs that create dedicated folders per backup type (Themes/, Files/, Products/, Customers/) directly into memory-monitored temporary staging before performing a single-pass ZipArchive compression with real-time SSE progress reporting.

2. High-Accuracy 0-Reference Scan Across Dynamic Themes

Shopify assets can be referenced either by raw filename, CDN URL, or GraphQL Global ID (GID) within schema settings and custom metafields. I engineered a cross-referencing regex and GID parser that checks all theme assets, metaobjects, and collections simultaneously, eliminating false-positive "0 reference" reports and allowing merchants to safely prune gigabytes of orphaned images.

3. Multi-Model AI SEO Orchestration with Graceful Fallbacks

To avoid dependency on a single AI provider and protect merchants from rate limits, I implemented a flexible dual-engine LLM pipeline. The system can run entirely locally through Ollama (e.g. Llama 3, Mistral) for privacy and zero API costs, or seamlessly switch to Gemini Pro for high-context marketing copy with automated retry handling.
